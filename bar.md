# Comparison bar

**Reference:** supplied `PERMAN · Campana — Water Levels and Climate` dashboard image, studied 2 November 2026. Measurements below are visual estimates.

1. A single compact header groups identity on the left and time/actions on the right; it remains one line at desktop size.
2. The first content row contains four equal, highly scannable metric cards with an uppercase label, a large reading, and one quiet contextual line.
3. The river forecast receives the largest chart surface and uses a dark forecast line over a low-contrast uncertainty band; observed data is visually distinct.
4. Supporting weather content is composed as independent white panels with a consistent hairline border, modest radius, and generous internal padding.
5. Forecasts favor columns of values and weather symbols over prose; high/low temperature is immediately legible before details.
6. Charts use pale gridlines, small axis labels, a short legend, and a title that names the measurement and timeframe.
7. The overall dashboard is restrained: large areas are pale neutral, navigation has no visual noise, and action buttons are limited to refresh and report delivery.
