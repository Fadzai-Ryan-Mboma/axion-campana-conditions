# Comparison bar

**Reference:** the supplied `PERMAN · Campana — Water Levels and Climate` dashboard image, studied 2 November 2026. It is the comparison bar for operational data hierarchy and chart legibility. Measurements below are visual estimates. AXION’s public site supplies brand character; Apple Weather, Windy, and Landingfolio inform patterns only.

1. A compact one-line header puts identity left, timestamp centre-right, and no more than three primary actions at the far right.
2. The first data row holds four same-height metric cards; each has one uppercase label, one large reading, and a short supporting line—no competing chart inside a card.
3. The river panel dominates the workspace; the measured past trace is muted, the active forecast trace is dark and continuous, and the uncertainty band is visually secondary.
4. Every chart names both its measure and time horizon, uses a labelled y-axis with the unit, and formats x-axis ticks as date plus local time at a scan-friendly interval.
5. Multi-series charts use a compact explicit legend directly above the plot; color and line style are both used to distinguish values.
6. Five-day forecast cards use a fixed, repeatable reading order: date, condition symbol, high/low, precipitation, then wind speed and direction.
7. Wind is presented as daily columns with both the maximum speed and dominant direction printed directly at the bar; severity color is constrained to the measurement—not decorative surfaces.
8. A single, labelled operational interval control changes the temporal resolution across plots and summaries; the active choice remains visually unambiguous and the current timezone is stated alongside freshness.
